MINOR

**A decision that is easy to take back no longer has to stop a slice.** The `decide` setting of `/cruise` gains three values, `provisional-shadow`, `provisional-advisory` and `provisional`. Under the first two the skipper still asks a person, and writes beside the entry what the stricter mode would have done; under `provisional` it takes an always-ask approval itself where the change is easy or guarded to reverse and none of `flag_default=yes`, `ci_workflow=yes` or `migrate_file=yes` holds, and never for a gate, a check or CI question, writes `Status: provisional · ratify by <date>` and a `Revert:` line naming the commits carrying the entry's `Decision: D<n>` trailer, and carries on. A new script, `scripts/provisional.py`, says what an always-ask item becomes under the `decide` setting and audits the log before `cruise: done`, and `check-decisions` reads the new `Status`, `Revert:` and `Provisional (shadow|advisory):` lines.

**Catch-up.** `slipwai migrate` brings the new `scripts/provisional.py`, the changed `scripts/check-decisions.py` and `scripts/agents/cruise.py`, and the regenerated `commands/cruise.md`, `commands/cruise-settings.md`, `agents/drive-skipper.md`, `agents/drive-implement.md` and `agents/drive-slice.md`, and nothing else asks anything of the project. The three new `decide` values, `provisional-shadow`, `provisional-advisory` and `provisional`, are off by default: `.specify/cruise.json` is untouched by `migrate`, gains no key, and keeps the value it had, so a run decides exactly as it did. The first iteration after upgrading appends one entry, to the log of the feature it works in, recording the `decide` mode it finds: the iteration runs `python3 scripts/agents/cruise.py mode` (the runner does not) and appends what it prints, and that entry is the baseline for every feature's log, the latest `When` across them. To use the new values, ask for them through `/cruise-settings`, which moves `decide` one rung at a time (`provisional-shadow`, then `provisional-advisory`, then `provisional`) and refuses a larger step: let an iteration record each rung before taking the next, because an iteration whose `decide` is more than one rung higher than the latest recorded mode parks, naming that entry and the way out. No iteration of a run ever sets `decide`: `--set decide=…` and `tell` refuse inside one, the `PreToolUse` guard refuses an editing tool aimed at `.specify/cruise.json` (by any name for the file, a hard link included), and the runner parks on any raise of `decide` it sees by another route — inside an iteration, between two, while parked, or behind a settings file that was broken and then mended — until a `told:` message; a person who changed `decide` by editing the file resumes with a `told:` message (`/cruise-tell`), whereas a raise through `/cruise-settings` while the runner runs queues that confirmation itself and parks nothing, and a step back never parks. A settings file that names `decide`, or any key, twice is refused, and `mode` does not read a mode entry dated after now; with several features and no `--feature` it prints the entry, numbered against every log, instead of failing. A mode entry you score by hand needs `migrate_file=yes`, because its `Written to` is `.specify/cruise.json`. Existing decisions logs pass unchanged: `make check-decisions` reads an entry with none of the new lines exactly as it did. Until a later release adds verbs for it, a provisional entry is ratified by hand: edit its `Status` to `ratified <date>` to keep the decision, or revert the commits carrying its `Decision: D<n>` trailer yourself and write `reverted <date>`. The run will not say `done` while an entry is unratified: it ends `cruise: parked: ratify D<n>` instead, naming the first, and only `provisional` writes such an entry, but an entry written under `provisional` still holds the run after a person steps back to `provisional-shadow` or further, until it is ratified or reverted. The owner brief `.specify/product-owner.md` is merged like any other file: a brief the project never edited takes the two new sentences, and one it edited keeps its own edits; `commands/cruise.md` is the authority on the shape of an entry.
