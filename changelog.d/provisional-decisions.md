**A decision that is easy to take back no longer has to stop a slice.** The `decide` setting of `/cruise` gains three values, `provisional-shadow`, `provisional-advisory` and `provisional`. Under the first two the skipper still asks a person, and writes beside the entry what the stricter mode would have done; under `provisional` it takes an always-ask approval itself where the change is easy or guarded to reverse, writes `Status: provisional · ratify by <date>` and a `Revert:` line naming the commits carrying the entry's `Decision: D<n>` trailer, and carries on. A new script, `scripts/provisional.py`, says what an always-ask item becomes under the `decide` setting and audits the log before `cruise: done`, and `check-decisions` reads the new `Status`, `Revert:` and `Provisional (shadow|advisory):` lines.

**Catch-up.** `slipwai migrate` brings the new `scripts/provisional.py`, the changed `scripts/check-decisions.py` and `scripts/agents/cruise.py`, and the regenerated `commands/cruise.md`, and nothing else asks anything of the project. The three new `decide` values, `provisional-shadow`, `provisional-advisory` and `provisional`, are off by default: `.specify/cruise.json` is untouched by `migrate`, gains no key, and keeps the value it had, so a run decides exactly as it did. To use them, ask for them through `/cruise-settings`, which moves `decide` one rung at a time (`provisional-shadow`, then `provisional-advisory`, then `provisional`) and refuses a larger step; no iteration of a run ever sets `decide`. Existing decisions logs pass unchanged: `make check-decisions` reads an entry with none of the new lines exactly as it did. Until a later release adds verbs for it, a provisional entry is ratified by hand: edit its `Status` to `ratified <date>` to keep the decision, or revert the commits carrying its `Decision: D<n>` trailer yourself and write `reverted <date>`. The run will not say `done` while an entry is unratified: it ends `cruise: parked: ratify D<n>` instead, naming the first, and it can only happen under `provisional`, because the other values never write a provisional entry. The owner brief `.specify/product-owner.md` is merged like any other file: a brief the project never edited takes the two new sentences, and one it edited keeps its own edits; `commands/cruise.md` is the authority on the shape of an entry.
