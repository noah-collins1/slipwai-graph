MINOR

**`make benchmark` now says how long a slice took from ready to accepted, and where the time went while nobody was working on it, instead of passing off the sum of its stage brackets as the slice's wall time.** The old `wall` figure was the brackets added up, so a slice that sat a day waiting for a dependency, a review or a merge read the same as one that did not, and a feature's slices, whose brackets overlap, summed to more time than had passed. The overview now carries three figures under three names: **elapsed**, which runs from the slice's ready commit to its accepted one and is read from git; **stage time**, which adds up the brackets and is what `wall` was; and a waiting table that splits elapsed by cause — dependency, worker, review, integration, and what no record accounts for — beside the slice's re-entered stages and cost. Each request a delegate made is now charged to one slice by the delegate's own description and the chain of delegates that spawned it, a cut-off entry's stage time ends at its last attributed line, every figure says where it was read, and a figure nothing could read says `unknown` with the reason and never a bare `0`. Decision health reads `unknown` until a decision carries a tier. The ladder gains a `gate` stage, the full gate run after a slice's merge, which the host brackets as the slice's own, and `/drive` and `/cruise` ask each `drive-slice` delegate to be described as `drive-slice <id>` so its requests can be charged to the slice.

**Catch-up.** `slipwai migrate` brings the new `scripts/agents/measures.py` and `scripts/agents/attribution.py`, the changed `scripts/agents/benchmark.py` and the regenerated `commands/drive.md` and `commands/cruise.md`, and nothing else asks anything of the project. In the overview and in `make benchmark`, `wall` → `stage time` (the same figure) and the `rework` column → `re-entered` (the same list of stages re-entered after implementation); in `benchmark.py --json`, `rework` is now an object `{seconds, tokens}` for the time and tokens spent re-entering stages, and the old list of stage names is under `reentered`, so a script that read `rework` as a list reads `reentered` instead. The `gate` stage is a new stage name that an `end` takes no signals for: bracket the full gate after a slice's merge with `python3 scripts/agents/benchmark.py start specs/<feature>/slices/<id> gate` and `end` as the regenerated `commands/drive.md` says, and describe each `drive-slice` delegate as `drive-slice <id>`; a record that has neither is read as it was, with its attribution unknown; no record is rewritten, no `project.json` key changes, `make check-benchmark` warns of nothing it did not warn of before, and nothing must be redone.
